## Design Context

### Users
Chattodo is for technical support, QA, developers, and team leads who handle screenshots, issue reports, logs, and follow-up tasks every day. They need a clear product introduction and usage documentation, not engineering setup instructions.

### Brand Personality
Calm, practical, trustworthy. The product should feel like a clean work tool for problem handling, with enough visual polish to feel deliberate but not like a marketing splash page.

### Aesthetic Direction
Use a light, mostly white interface. Avoid dark feature bands, heavy card grids, excessive borders, and large product screenshots on the landing page. The website should introduce the product concept and workflow, not showcase the app UI as the main content. Use subtle blue accents, soft surface tinting, rhythm, and typography to reduce emptiness while preserving a flat, professional style.

### Design Principles
1. Keep the landing page informational: explain value, workflow, audience, and use cases without product UI demos.
2. Preserve a white primary palette with restrained blue accents and soft neutral surfaces.
3. Prefer flat layouts, dividers, spacing rhythm, and typographic hierarchy over boxed cards.
4. Reduce visual emptiness by using subtle section backgrounds, alignment anchors, and compact vertical rhythm.
5. Documentation should read as user-facing usage guidance, not developer run/build instructions.
